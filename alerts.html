How a warning escalates

72 h · Watch
Signal seen in the coarse forecast. Advisory officers are informed.
48 h · Alert
Downscaled forecast pins the risk to specific Panchayats.
24 h · Warning
Messages go out in local languages with crop-specific actions.
6 h · Act now
Voice calls and loudspeaker text reach households without smartphones.
