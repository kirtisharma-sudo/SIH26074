The problem, the idea, the outcome.

THE PROBLEM

Coarse spatial resolution

Operational weather forecasts are built for large grid cells, not for the boundary of a single Panchayat.

THE IDEA

AI-powered spatial downscaling

Terrain, land cover, vegetation, history and ground observations refine that coarse forecast into a local one.

THE OUTCOME

Actionable Panchayat-level intelligence

A forecast specific enough to change what a farmer does today, not just what a district expects this week.
