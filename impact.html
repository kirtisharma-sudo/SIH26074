Where it plugs in

Block-level agromet advisories already reach farmers through IMD's agromet services. This system adds a Panchayat layer beneath them: same bulletins, sharper location, plus alerts and a planner farmers can query.

Honest limits

Downscaling cannot invent storms the coarse forecast missed. Accuracy varies by terrain, and sparse ground stations limit validation in remote areas. Every Panchayat forecast should carry its uncertainty, and advisories should be reviewed by agromet officers.
